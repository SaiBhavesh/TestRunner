How it works

1

You describe the test

Share your page link and what you want checked, such as “can a new visitor sign up?”.

2

We screen the page

An automated browser confirms the page loads and records what it sees before any person is paid.

3

You get a quote

A person prices the study, usually within about an hour. You pay only after you see the price.

4

Testers try it

Recruited testers follow your task and write what happened.

5

You read the report

Findings are ranked by severity, with each tester’s own words attached.

Pricing

Quoted per study

Every study is priced by a person after a quick feasibility check, so you see the exact cost before you pay.
